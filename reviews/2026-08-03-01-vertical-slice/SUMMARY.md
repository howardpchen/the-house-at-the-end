# Summary

Version 0.1.0 establishes an original Pebble-native game rather than a source
fork. It includes a complete design and narrative plan, a platform-independent
state engine, checksummed persistence, elapsed-time guest production, house and
construction screens, and the first supply-limited hostile memory.

Host rules tests and a clean universal SDK compilation pass. The live Emery progression
pass exercised gathering, two hearth upgrades, first guest arrival, all three
structures, both guest roles, ration preparation, the Crooked Hall expedition,
its hostile echo, and memory completion. Basalt is built into the same PBW and
receives a separate minimum-size layout pass.

The first Emery screenshot exposed clipping in the verbose status line. The
line was replaced with a compact `K/M/R/C` form, rebuilt, and recaptured. One
intermediate capture also caught a partial redraw while the per-second tick was
updating; the immediate recheck is the canonical guest screenshot.

Basalt then exposed a three-line wrap in the expedition HUD. Its supply counters
were compacted to `C/R`, rebuilt, reinstalled, and recaptured on both platforms.
The reviewed 25,573-byte PBW has SHA-256
`f53938cf4c384e8e64ada43fa587662ab9a8e40c71ec5a47e90cc03d22d51000`.

Physical Time Steel and Time 2 installation are not part of this review. Button
feel, real-device persistence, and extended stability remain hardware checks.
